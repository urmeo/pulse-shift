Shared data

Join Capital Bikeshare rides, NOAA DCA weather and CAMS AQI by UTC hour, with EPA daily AQI as the historical fallback. The 2022–2024 panel has 26,289 hours.

Expected rides use a 2022–2023 baseline for each season, weekday/weekend group and hour. Keep hours with at least 20 expected rides; low demand means actual rides below half that count. This leaves 24,354 active hours and 12 weather, AQI and time features.

Historical evaluation

Fit five model variants on 16,150 active hours from 2022–2023; test on 8,204 active hours from 2024.

Climatology uses the training low-demand rate for each season, day type and hour. AUROC measures ranking. The unweighted model's 95% interval is 0.900–0.967, from 1,000 whole-day bootstrap resamples. Its mean prediction was 6.88%, compared with 2.46% observed; probabilities overestimated the 2024 event rate.

The time-shift simulation uses the unweighted model's 2024 test estimates and recorded conditions. It compares hours within ±3 hours on the same day, requiring heat index below 103°F and AQI below 150 at the chosen hour. Net modeled change includes cancellation and shift losses. Shifted demand is assumed to transfer; actual benefits were not measured.

Browser use

Separately refit unweighted logistic regression on all 24,354 active hours from 2022–2024. Export fixed scaling parameters and coefficients to estimate the chance of low demand from manual inputs or DC forecasts. The historical scores above evaluate the earlier fit.

Forecasts use Open-Meteo weather and CAMS AQI. Compare complete hours in the next 24 hours, from 06:00 to 21:00 DC time, with heat index below 103°F and AQI below 150. Choose the lowest estimated probability. These are study limits; follow official advisories. Live forecast accuracy has not been prospectively evaluated.

Other research

AQI and smoke analyses examine daily and hourly associations in the DC records. Different exposures and controls limit comparison; these observational analyses do not establish causality.

The Seoul analysis fits a separate 10-feature model on its own UCI dataset. Within each season, train on the earliest 75% of functioning dates and test on the latest 25%, keeping whole dates together.

Research checks do not change the browser parameters. The project estimates aggregate network demand, not individual activity or health outcomes.

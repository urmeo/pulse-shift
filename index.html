Build the browser model

Join Capital Bikeshare rides, NOAA DCA weather and CAMS AQI by UTC hour, with EPA daily AQI as the historical fallback. The 2022–2024 panel has 26,288 hours.

Expected rides use the 2022–2023 baseline for each season, weekday/weekend group and hour. Keep expected counts ≥20; low demand means rides below half the expected count. This leaves 24,354 active hours and 12 weather, AQI and time features.

Refit unweighted logistic regression on all 24,354 active hours. Export the scaler, weights and intercept as fixed browser parameters.

Estimate new conditions

For each new hour, derive 12 features, apply the fixed scaler and compute the logistic probability. Manual conditions give one estimate.

Open-Meteo weather and CAMS AQI forecasts let the app compare complete hours in the next 24 hours, from 06:00 to 21:00 DC time. Forecast selection requires heat index below 103°F and AQI below 150, then chooses the lowest estimated probability.

Historical test

Fit five model variants on 16,150 hours from 2022–2023; test on 8,204 hours from 2024. Two model scores are listed below. This test evaluates a separate fit from the all-years browser refit.

Unweighted logistic0.936

Climatology predicts the training low-demand rate for each season, day type and hour. AUROC measures ranking. The unweighted model's 95% interval is 0.900–0.967, from 1,000 whole-day bootstrap resamples.

Its mean prediction is 6.88%, compared with 2.46% observed on the 2024 test. Its probabilities overestimate the event rate.

Simulated time shifts

Use the historical model's 2024 test estimates and recorded conditions to compare hours within ±3 hours on the same day, under the study's heat and AQI limits. Predicted ride recovery assumes demand transfers to the chosen hour.

AQI and smoke

Use applicable 2022–2024 DC records for daily and hourly associations, exposure curves, smoke context and measurement-error scenarios. Different exposures and controls limit comparison; these observational analyses do not establish causality.

Separate Seoul refit

Fit a city-specific 10-feature model on its own UCI dataset. Within each season, train on the earliest 75% of functioning dates and test on the latest 25%, keeping whole dates together.

Limits and recorded outputs

This prototype estimates aggregate bike-share demand. Individual activity and health outcomes were not measured. Live forecast accuracy and actual time-shift benefits remain unmeasured.

Research checks vary features, labels, subgroups, policy windows and smoke thresholds. Tables, curves and predictions record the results. Metadata include the panel checksum, source hashes and runtime versions.
